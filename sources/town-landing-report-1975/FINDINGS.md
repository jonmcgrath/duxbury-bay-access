## 1. Where the definitions came from
The 2011 Bay Management Plan wording for "town landing" and "way to the water" is copied almost word for word from this report (report p. 2–3). The 1975 version adds that town landings "are either historic, or created by enabling legislation enacted by the Massachusetts State Legislature in 1908". The 1908 Committee on Town Landings report (Town Report 1908 p. 43) says Duxbury asked for that law.

## 3. The six historic landings (1899 Report on Town Landings, Town Report for 1898, p. 38)
Committee: Laurence Bradford and Hamilton Wadsworth, Jan 21, 1899. Accepted March 6, 1899; plans recorded and stone bounds set (bounds delayed, per the 1900 report).
| 1899 # | Description (1899) | Earliest date in town books | This project |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | At Powder Point End by new bridge | Laid out by County Commissioners Jan 1870; recorded as a "Highway to the Sea" (1899 vote) | 06/07 Powder Point |
| 2 | Foot of lane, land formerly of Reuben and Clark Peterson, Duck Hill River | 1834 | 05 Peterson's |
| 3 | Foot of lane through land formerly of Simeon Soule, Duck Hill River | 1803 and 1834 | 04 Simeon Soule's |
| 4 | Foot of lane through land of E. F. Adams, near former Drew's Salt Works | 1834 | 03 Drew Salt Works |
| 5 | Old Cove, by new town road to Abram's Hill | 1834. "The most valuable one that the town possesses" | 02 Old Cove |
| 6 | At Mill Pond, by Blue Fish River, by land formerly of Amherst A. Frazar | **1715**, re-defined 1822 and 1834. "Believed … to be the oldest landing in the town" | 09 Bluefish? See open question A |

Deeds cited in the 1899 report:
- **Old Cove:** Plymouth Deeds **Book 215, Page 28**, Dec 30, 1843, Town of Duxbury to George Loring. Part of the landing was conveyed, and "the present landing place shall be forever reserved to the town" (town meeting Nov 13, 1843).
- **Mill Pond (#6):** part sold to Samuel A. Frazar and others, Dec 30, 1840, Plymouth Deeds **Book 201, Page 87** (town meetings Nov 23 and Dec 7, 1840).
- Jan 3, 1835 town meeting declined to sell any landings.
- The 1899 committee excluded "the landing place at Captain's Hill, traded off to Stephen M. Allen".

## 4. Site-by-site votes and records (as cited in the 1975 report)
- **10 Mattakeeset Ct:** 1902/1903 vote to build the highway; 1916 land exchange with Wallace C. Flagg (100 ft more frontage), deeds passed by 1917; 1920 $150 for landing and bathing beach; 1921 sea wall completed.
- **02 Old Cove:** 1923 Art. 35 tabled; **March 1, 1924** voted to exchange land with Helen Ripley Clapp and Clara Ripley Evans (Town Report 1924 p. 72).
- **09 Bluefish River (Old Mill Dam):** 1900 Art. 11 wharf near Blue River Bridge (J. H. Killian offered his part); 1904 engine-house article; **1916** voted to move the southerly line stones 2 ft north (Town Report 1916 p. 90).
- **01 Ford's Stand:** 1897–98 highway from Cut Island to the Hummock accepted, $500; 1931 Art. 16 $10,000 Gurnet Rd extension to Pine Point. *No landing layout cited.*
- **06/07 Powder Point:** 1899 "Highway to the Sea"; 1902 F. B. Knapp quitclaim reversed; 1916 King Caesar Rd junction improvement; 1922 Cedar Pond landing article postponed.
- **14 Harden Hill:** 1941 Art. 32 named "Hardin Hill Road" (Town Report 1941 p. 136); 1904 p. 58 layout change through Sisters of St. Margaret land.
- **17 Hicks Point and 16 Landing Road:** 1906 report recommends both; **March 8, 1909** voted that Selectmen lay out a landing at Hicks Point "and another through the property of Mrs. Foote", $500. Town plan dated 9/12/09 for Landing Road.
- **15 Howland's:** 1895 Art. 10; **1896** voted to accept "the road and landing near Allen's Wharf … as laid out by the Selectmen"; 1915 road repairs; 1931 Art. 40 named Howland's Lane. **This conflicts with the NSRWA claim that the town bought it in 1894.** *Unresolved.*
- **13 Jocelyn ("Josselyn Avenue"):** March 4, 1907 highway accepted over land of John E. Josselyn.
- **12 Water St:** 1899 highway near James K. Burgess house accepted "providing there is no land damage"; 1900–01 extension to the shore accepted; land given by Charles C. Clapp.
- **11 Winsor St:** 1908 Selectmen took steps to establish a landing after public bathing rights were questioned; widening 1919–1922 (tabled 1922).
- **WW-01 Shipyard Lane:** Eben H. Ellison deeded the land in 1943. Accepted unanimously under **Article 24, 1944** (Town Report 1944 p. 108). The deed is bounded "southeasterly by low water mark in Duxbury Bay". **Restrictions:** recreational use only; "restricted to the use of the residents of Duxbury and their guests"; no pier. *This confirms the gift and dates it. Deed book/page not given.*
- **WW-02–WW-05 Elder Brewster, Samoset, Sagamore, Massasoit:** accepted as ways to the shore, laid out Feb 11, 1907 (Town Report 1907 p. 52); 1920 Massasoit Rd and Mayflower Ave laid out to near the Standish cellar; 1941 Art. 36 Samoset Rd laid out. The 1975 committee said town funds improved these to high water in 1907–08, that some were wrongly posted private, and asked for a Standish Shore committee.
- **WW-06 Miles Standish homesite:** accepted **March 1, 1930, Art. 15**, from the Standish Monument Association. The committee said it "currently qualifies as a landing".
- **WW-09 Bay Farm:** acquired 1972 Art. 21 (Block P lots 25, 11, 25-11, 25-J; $200,000; vote 413–97). Land Court Plan 14792-6.
- **WW-10 Island Creek Pond:** "the Town's only documented fresh water way"; Town Plan Book 2 p. 111.
- **Not in the 1975 report:** WW-07 Longview, WW-08 Elderberry.

## 5. Open questions this raises
- **A. St. George Street vs. Bluefish River.** The 1975 report lists **16 shore landings**: Mattakeeset, Old Cove, Drew, Simeon Soule, Peterson, Powder Point (one site, both sides), **St. George Street (by Old Mill Pond)**, **Old Mill (Blue Fish River, by Old Mill Dam)**, Winsor, Water, Josselyn, Hardin Hill, Howlands, Landing Road, Hicks Point, Ford's Stand. It ties 1899 #6 to **St. George Street**. This project has no St. George Street site but has two Powder Point sites (06 and 07). So either 06 Powder Point Landing is misplaced or the project is missing St. George St. Check against Plan Book 2 p. 6. *Our history.json puts the 1715 landing on 09 Bluefish; that may belong to St. George St.*
- **B. The "16 town landings" count** in the 2011 plan probably matches this list of 16, which includes Howland's. *Assumed.*
- **C. 1975 recommendation format** (parking limits, foot landings) is a useful baseline for comparing current conditions.
