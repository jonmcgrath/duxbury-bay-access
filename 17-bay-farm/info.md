# Bay Farm

**Category:** Town Landing
**ID:** 17
**Address:** 31 Loring Street, Duxbury/Kingston MA
**Coordinates:** 42.003, -70.713 *(approximate)*

## Parking
25 spaces; Viewing and Recreational Access

## Notes
80-acre conservation area; Duxbury/Kingston town line; sandy beach, tide pools, salt marshes, woodlands; Southern terminus of Bay Circuit Trail; jointly owned by Duxbury, Kingston, DEP
