# Bay Farm

**Category:** Town Landing
**ID:** 17
**Address:** 31 Loring Street, Duxbury/Kingston MA
**Coordinates:** 42.005086, -70.711988

## Parking
~25 spaces

## Notes
80-acre conservation area on the Duxbury/Kingston town line. Sandy beach, tide pools, salt marshes, woodlands. Southern terminus of Bay Circuit Trail. Jointly owned by Duxbury, Kingston, and MA DEP.
