# Mattakeeset Town Pier

**Category:** Town Landing
**ID:** 10
**Address:** 35 Mattakeeset Court, Duxbury MA
**Coordinates:** 42.039021, -70.669986

## Parking
60 cars · 12 trailers · 4 handicapped

## Notes
Primary hub. Public boat launch ramp. ADA accessible. Seasonal restrooms, benches, trash. Snug Harbor between Duxbury Yacht Club and DBMS. Dawn to dusk. Harbormaster: (781) 934-2866.
