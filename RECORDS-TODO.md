# Records still to find (as of 2026-11-11)

Everything that can be found online has been searched: the 1642–1770 town records, the 1975 Town Landing Study Committee report, and every Duxbury Annual Town Report on Internet Archive from 1879 to 2014. What is left needs the **Plymouth County Registry of Deeds**, the **Town Clerk**, or a report that is not online. Process and full citations: `sources/duxbury-annual-reports/FINDINGS.md`.

Legend: **[R]** Registry (TitleView online or 50 Obery St, Plymouth) · **[TC]** Town Clerk · **[L]** library or archive · *assumed* = not yet checked against the record itself.

- [ ] **[R] Bay Farm deeds, 1972**, and Land Court Certificate 47518 / Plan 14792-6 (WW-09).

## 2. Layouts the town accepted but whose plans are with the Town Clerk
- [ ] **[TC] Town Plan Book 1**: p. 4 Mattakeeset Ct, p. 5 Blue Fish River, p. 16 Howland's, p. 17 Hardin Hill, pp. 70–71 Standish Shore, p. 75.
- [ ] **[TC] Town Plan Book 2**: p. 111 Island Creek Pond way, p. 113 Water St extension, p. 144 Standish homestead and Shore Way, p. 147 "Town Landings as recorded at Plymouth", p. 148 Landing Road (9/12/09).
- [ ] **[TC] Layout plans for the 1908 Water St landing and the two Sept 2, 1909 landings** (16 Foote; 17 Weston, Chandler and Loring Co.). Plan Book 2 p. 148 is probably the Foote one.
- [ ] **[TC] "Duxbury Deeds Book 2"**: the town kept its own deed books (the 1901 oyster grant is "recorded in Duxbury Deeds Book 2, pages 366 and 367"). Ask whether landing deeds were copied there.
- [ ] **[TC] Original town records** Vol. a pp. 97, 99, 300 and Record No. 5 pp. 100–101 (1715–1768), plus the 1803, 1822 and 1834 landing definitions and the Nov 13, 1843 and Nov 23 / Dec 7, 1840 town meetings. The 1770–1878 town books are not online.

## 3. Reports not online
- [ ] **[L] Annual Town Reports 1916–1919.** They are not on Internet Archive or at the State Library. They hold the 1916 Flagg exchange vote and the 1916 Blue Fish River boundary vote, both cited now only from the 1975 report. Ask the Duxbury Free Library or the Town Clerk.
- [ ] **[L] 1870–1879 volume** on Internet Archive: its OCR would not download. Read it page by page for the Jan 1870 Powder Point layout.
- [ ] **[L] The 1922 Town Counsel opinion** on rights to the shore and town landings (voted 1922). It was not found in the 1922–1925 reports.
