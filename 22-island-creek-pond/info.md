# Island Creek Pond

**Category:** Town Landing
**ID:** 22
**Address:** 287 Tobey Garden Street, Duxbury MA
**Coordinates:** 42.02414, -70.712438

## Parking
Large lot — free

## Notes
Freshwater Great Pond (43-acre Crocker Memorial Park). Canoe/kayak launch from shore. Herring migration history and Native American heritage. Dawn to dusk.
